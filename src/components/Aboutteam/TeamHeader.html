Where it all started :

Our story started on the campus of Sunway University, where four coursemates kept bumping into each other between lectures and coffee runs. What began as shared assignments and late-night study sessions grew into a shared idea — building a platform for people who, just like us, simply love a good drink. Different majors, different strengths, one project we're proud to call ours.
