Personal Bio

I focus on building intuitive, clean, and interactive digital interfaces. My goal is to bridge the gap between aesthetics and function, ensuring that the structural engineering behind the screen translates to a seamless user experience in front of it.

Over the past few years, I have collaborated with design and technical teams to ship web features, design interfaces, and refine application logic. I enjoy solving challenges that push me to learn new technologies and workflows.
